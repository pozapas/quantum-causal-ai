## Learn

A quantum channel maps input density operators to output density operators. A deterministic physical channel is completely positive and trace preserving. Complete positivity means the map remains positive when an untouched reference system is included. Positivity on isolated inputs alone is insufficient.

Kraus operators provide one representation of a channel. Apply each operator on both sides of the input state, then add the results. The trace-preservation condition is a sum of conjugate products equal to the identity. Different Kraus lists can represent the same channel.

A channel can erase information even though total system and environment evolution is reversible. Tracing out the environment hides its record. A channel therefore differs from a unitary gate on the system alone. Preparation, damping, and measurement without a retained outcome are useful examples.

:::{important} Key relation
:class: qcai-equation
E(rho) = sum\_k K\_k rho K\_k†; sum\_k K\_k† K\_k = I. Dagger means conjugate transpose. This condition gives a trace-preserving Kraus map.
:::

:::{tip} Worked example
A bit-flip channel uses K0 = sqrt(0.9) I and K1 = sqrt(0.1) X. The conjugate products sum to 0.9I + 0.1I = I. On \|0&gt;&lt;0\|, the output has probabilities 0.9 for 0 and 0.1 for 1.
:::

## Practice

:::{admonition} Exercise
:class: tip

A bit-flip channel has flip probability 0.25. Apply it to \|1&gt;&lt;1\| and give its output diagonal.
:::

:::{dropdown} Solution

The system stays in \|1&gt; with probability 0.75 and flips to \|0&gt; with probability 0.25. In basis order 0,1, the diagonal is (0.25,0.75).
:::

## Check your understanding

**Which condition guarantees trace preservation in a Kraus representation?**

- **A.** The Kraus operators sum to I
- **B.** The sum of K dagger K equals I
- **C.** Every Kraus operator is unitary

:::{dropdown} Show the answer
**B. The sum of K dagger K equals I**

The conjugate-product sum must equal the identity. Individual Kraus operators can be nonunitary, and their ordinary sum has no such requirement.
:::

:::{caution} Scope and assumptions
The channel assumes no relevant initial correlation between the input system and an unmodelled environment for this fixed-map description.
:::
