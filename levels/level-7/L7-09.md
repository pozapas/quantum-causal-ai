## Learn

Choose one narrow question with an identifiable target. State the proposed contribution relative to a named baseline. A useful first project can compare experiment selection strategies for two or more channel families. Avoid claiming a universal quantum causal learner from one small example.

Write a protocol before tuning. Include hypotheses, process family, allowed instruments, parameter range, resource budget, metric, uncertainty method, and rejection criteria. Check the protocol with an analytic toy case. Then run a controlled pilot and preserve all attempts, including failures.

The final report should separate established results, numerical evidence, and interpretation. A negative result can reveal an identification failure or a cost omitted from earlier comparisons. Publish enough details for replication. Label any proposal beyond the measured regime as a future research question.

:::{important} Key relation
:class: qcai-equation
Example target: p in E\_p(rho)=(1-p)rho+pI/2. With \|0&gt; input and Z measurement, P(1)=p/2, so p\_hat=2 times the observed fraction of outcomes 1.
:::

:::{tip} Worked example
For 1000 shots, suppose 120 outcomes are 1. The estimate is p\_hat=0.24. Under the model, the variance of this unbiased estimator is 4q(1-q)/1000, where q=p/2. A confidence procedure and physical-range handling must be specified before reporting an interval.
:::

## Practice

:::{admonition} Exercise
:class: tip

For 800 shots and 80 outcomes 1, estimate p. Name a residual pattern that would challenge the assumed depolarizing model.
:::

:::{dropdown} Solution

The observed fraction is 0.1, so p\_hat is 0.2. Different contraction factors across calibrated X, Y, and Z probes would challenge isotropic depolarization and suggest a broader noise model.
:::

## Check your understanding

**What makes the project falsifiable?**

- **A.** A stated observation that would reject its model or claim
- **B.** Only a positive expected outcome
- **C.** An unspecified future experiment

:::{dropdown} Show the answer
**A. A stated observation that would reject its model or claim**

A falsifiable protocol defines observations that count against the model or proposed improvement. It also preserves the analysis plan when the result is unfavorable.
:::

:::{caution} Scope and assumptions
The estimator assumes the specific depolarizing convention and trusted preparation and readout. Bias in those operations can bias p\_hat.
:::
