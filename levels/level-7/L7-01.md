## Learn

Quantum machine learning uses quantum methods in a learning procedure or learns from quantum data. Causal AI asks questions about interventions and mechanisms. Quantum causal inference studies causal questions about quantum systems. Their intersection is a research area, not one established universal algorithm.

Specify the input as classical records, quantum states, or access to a physical process. Specify the requested output as a label, expectation, channel estimate, graph, or causal effect. These choices determine the loss and the access assumptions. A classification score is not automatically a causal result.

A useful project statement names the estimand, data-generation mechanism, available interventions, and resource budget. An estimand is the quantity the study seeks to determine. A learning method can estimate it only if the model and experiment make that quantity identifiable.

:::{important} Key relation
:class: qcai-equation
Prediction risk is R(f) = E\[loss(f(X),Y)\]. A causal target can instead be tau = E\[Y\|do(A=1)\] - E\[Y\|do(A=0)\]. X,Y,A are explicitly defined classical variables here.
:::

:::{tip} Worked example
A circuit predicts a medical label from observational features. High test accuracy assesses prediction for that data distribution. It does not identify a treatment effect. A treatment-effect claim additionally needs a causal design, suitable assumptions, and a target such as the stated average intervention contrast.
:::

## Practice

:::{admonition} Exercise
:class: tip

A quantum model predicts laboratory outcomes from instrument settings. State what extra information is needed before calling its learned graph a causal discovery result.
:::

:::{dropdown} Solution

Specify the candidate process family, available interventions, hidden-system assumptions, and identifiability conditions. Check that distinct claimed graphs have distinct allowed predictions. Prediction accuracy alone cannot establish those requirements.
:::

## Check your understanding

**Which item defines a causal research target?**

- **A.** A precise intervention quantity and its assumptions
- **B.** The use of a quantum processor
- **C.** A high classification score alone

:::{dropdown} Show the answer
**A. A precise intervention quantity and its assumptions**

The target and its identification assumptions determine the causal question. A processor choice or prediction score alone does not supply them.
:::

:::{caution} Scope and assumptions
The phrase Quantum Causal AI covers several connected research directions. This course does not treat them as one settled technical definition.
:::
