## Learn

A fraction describes a part relative to a whole. The numerator is the count of the selected part. The denominator is the total count in the whole. State what belongs to the whole before you calculate.

If three of twelve counters are blue, the blue fraction is three over twelve. This is the same number as one quarter. Dividing both counts by three changes the written form but keeps the same proportion.

A fraction without its group can mislead. Three blue counters among twelve differ from three blue counters among six. The selected count is the same, but the second group has a larger blue fraction because its total is smaller.

:::{important} Key relation
:class: qcai-equation
fraction = selected count / total count. The total must be greater than zero.
:::

:::{tip} Worked example
A tray has eight apples and two pears. There are ten fruits in all. The pear fraction is 2/10, which equals 1/5. The apple fraction is 8/10. The two fractions add to one because the groups cover the tray.
:::

## Practice

:::{admonition} Exercise
:class: tip

A box has six red blocks and nine blue blocks. Find the red fraction and simplify it.
:::

:::{dropdown} Solution

The total is fifteen blocks. The red fraction is 6/15. Divide numerator and denominator by three to get 2/5. The denominator counts all blocks, not only blue blocks.
:::

## Check your understanding

**A class has five left-handed pupils among twenty pupils. What is the fraction?**

- **A.** 5/15
- **B.** 20/5
- **C.** 5/20

:::{dropdown} Show the answer
**C. 5/20**

The selected count is five and the whole class contains twenty. The fraction therefore uses five over twenty, or one quarter.
:::

:::{caution} Scope and assumptions
A proportion describes a specified group. It does not automatically describe another population.
:::
