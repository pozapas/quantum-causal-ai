## Learn

Quantum physics gives rules for physical systems such as light and atoms. These rules explain experimental results that simple everyday pictures miss. We will learn their mathematics step by step instead of using the word quantum to mean mysterious.

Causal reasoning asks how actions can change results. Artificial intelligence builds systems that perform tasks such as prediction or classification. A system can predict well and still give a poor answer about the result of an action.

Quantum Causal AI is a meeting place for research questions across these fields. It is not one finished tool with a guaranteed benefit. You will first learn each field, then study carefully defined combinations and the evidence needed to evaluate them.

:::{important} Key relation
:class: qcai-equation
Three questions: how does a system behave; what changes a result; what can a model learn?
:::

:::{tip} Worked example
A researcher records results from a light experiment. Quantum physics helps model the experiment. Causal reasoning helps plan which device setting to change. A learning algorithm can fit a model to records. Each part has a different job and check.
:::
