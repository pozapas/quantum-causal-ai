## Learn

A state describes how something is at one time. A lamp can be on or off. A cup can be empty or full. Write the time with the state when you record a change.

An action is something you do. You can press a lamp switch and then look at the lamp. Record both the action and the result. This gives more information than a record of the result alone.

Two events can happen one after the other without one causing the other. A bird can sing before a lamp comes on. That order alone does not show that the bird controls the lamp.

:::{important} Key relation
:class: qcai-equation
Before: off. Action: press the switch. After: on.
:::

:::{tip} Worked example
A working battery lamp is off. You press its switch once, and it comes on. You record the starting state, your action, and the final state. Repeat with the same lamp to check whether the pattern continues.
:::

## Practice

:::{admonition} Exercise
:class: tip

A toy car is still. You push it, and it moves. Write a before, action, and after record.
:::

:::{dropdown} Solution

Before: the toy car is still. Action: you push the car. After: the car moves. This record states the observed sequence without claiming that all motion has the same cause.
:::

## Check your understanding

**What does a before-and-after record show first?**

- **A.** Every possible cause
- **B.** A law for all objects
- **C.** The states and their order

:::{dropdown} Show the answer
**C. The states and their order**

The record gives the observed states and their order. More evidence is necessary to compare causes or rule out other changes.
:::

:::{caution} Scope and assumptions
One before-and-after record is not a controlled causal experiment.
:::
