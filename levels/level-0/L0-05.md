## Learn

An outcome is a result of a trial. A trial is one try. If a closed bag holds red and blue counters, a draw can give either colour. Before the draw, the result is uncertain.

Possible means that a result can occur. Certain means that it must occur under the stated conditions. If every counter is red, a counter drawn from that bag must be red, provided the bag has not changed.

Uncertain does not mean that anything can happen. A bag with only red and blue counters cannot give a green counter under the model. State the possible outcomes before you make a prediction or run a trial.

:::{important} Key relation
:class: qcai-equation
Possible outcomes: red or blue. Impossible in this bag: green.
:::

:::{tip} Worked example
A bag contains two red counters and one blue counter. You mix them and draw one without looking. Red is possible, and blue is possible. Green is not possible because no green counter is in the bag.
:::

## Practice

:::{admonition} Exercise
:class: tip

A bag has four yellow counters and no other counters. Is a yellow draw possible, certain, or impossible? What about blue?
:::

:::{dropdown} Solution

Yellow is certain and is therefore also possible under the stated conditions. Blue is impossible because there is no blue counter in the unchanged bag.
:::

## Check your understanding

**A bag contains red and blue counters. What can you claim before one unseen draw?**

- **A.** Blue must occur
- **B.** Either listed colour can occur
- **C.** Green must occur

:::{dropdown} Show the answer
**B. Either listed colour can occur**

Both colours are present, so either can occur. The list of possible outcomes does not determine this single draw.
:::

:::{caution} Scope and assumptions
The statement assumes that the bag contents are known and unchanged.
:::
