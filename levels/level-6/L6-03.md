## Learn

A directed acyclic graph records candidate causal connections without directed cycles. In a quantum model, arrows can carry quantum systems. A node can represent a laboratory with input and output spaces. The graph alone does not specify its channel or measurement probabilities.

A quantum Markov condition connects the graph with a restricted operator structure. The exact condition depends on the formalism. Independent mechanisms and local operational choices must be defined. Do not replace all scalar probabilities in a classical factorization by arbitrary matrices and multiply them blindly.

An absent arrow states a restriction on possible influence within the model. An observed zero correlation does not prove an absent arrow. A mechanism can cancel or hide an influence for particular preparations. Model checks therefore require suitable interventions or a stated faithfulness assumption.

:::{important} Key relation
:class: qcai-equation
For a simple chain A -&gt; B -&gt; C, the intervention channel is E\_C\|A = E\_C\|B composed with E\_B\|A. This equation assumes no additional memory path or hidden common cause.
:::

:::{tip} Worked example
A preparation feeds a qubit through a dephasing channel B and then an X-basis measurement C. Complete dephasing makes the outcomes independent of an input relative phase. This independence is a property of that channel. It does not prove that the physical wire from A is absent.
:::

## Practice

:::{admonition} Exercise
:class: tip

A chain model predicts channel composition. Name two features that would make that simple composition description incomplete.
:::

:::{dropdown} Solution

A hidden environment that retains memory across the two stages can break the description. An unmodelled common cause of the laboratories can also require a larger joint process.
:::

## Check your understanding

**Does one zero correlation prove that a causal edge is absent?**

- **A.** Yes, by normalization
- **B.** No, a mechanism can hide the influence
- **C.** Yes, if the state is mixed

:::{dropdown} Show the answer
**B. No, a mechanism can hide the influence**

A present mechanism can give zero dependence for a particular state or setting. Structural conclusions require additional assumptions or informative interventions.
:::

:::{caution} Scope and assumptions
The Allen and Costa-Shrapnel formalisms use related but distinct definitions. This lesson does not assert one universal quantum Markov factorization.
:::
