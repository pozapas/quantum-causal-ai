Build the mathematical tools that causal and quantum models use. Work with numbers, vectors, functions, and uncertainty. Each calculation has a stated meaning. A correct calculation does not by itself prove that a model describes a physical system.
:::

## Lessons in this level

| Lesson | Objective | Lab |
|---|---|---|
| [L3-01 · Complex numbers and phase](#lesson-l3-01) | Calculate a complex magnitude and explain the difference between magnitude and phase. | [Interference](../../labs/04-interference.ipynb) |

| [L3-03 · Matrices as transformations](#lesson-l3-03) | Apply a matrix to a vector and test whether a simple transformation preserves norm. | [Qubits and the Bloch sphere](../../labs/05-qubit-bloch.ipynb) |
| [L3-04 · Tensor products and joint systems](#lesson-l3-04) | Construct a joint vector and distinguish a product state from a correlated state. | [Entanglement and Bell tests](../../labs/06-entanglement-bell.ipynb) |

| [L3-10 · Estimation, intervals, and repeated tests](#lesson-l3-10) | Estimate a probability and describe the sampling meaning of a confidence interval. | [Chance and repeated trials](../../labs/01-chance.ipynb) |
