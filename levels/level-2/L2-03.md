## Learn

A coordinate plot places one quantity on each axis. Each point represents a recorded pair. Read the axis labels, units, and scale before describing a trend. A steep-looking line can result from an axis range rather than a large effect.

A positive association means that larger values of one quantity tend to occur with larger values of the other in the records. It does not establish an arrow of causation. Shared causes, reverse influence, or selection can produce an association.

Interpolation estimates within the observed range. Extrapolation estimates beyond that range. A straight line can be useful locally but fail far away. Keep the data range visible and state where a prediction depends on an untested extension of the model.

:::{important} Key relation
:class: qcai-equation
Slope = change in vertical quantity / change in horizontal quantity, with both units stated.
:::

:::{tip} Worked example
A line passes through two recorded points: one hour and four tasks, then three hours and ten tasks. Its slope is (10-4)/(3-1)=3 tasks per hour. This summary does not show that adding any hour always causes three completed tasks.
:::

## Practice

:::{admonition} Exercise
:class: tip

A line passes through (2,5) and (6,13). Find its slope. If all data lie between x=2 and x=6, classify a prediction at x=20.
:::

:::{dropdown} Solution

The slope is (13-5)/(6-2)=8/4=2 vertical units per horizontal unit. A prediction at x=20 is extrapolation because twenty is outside the observed input range.
:::

## Check your understanding

**What does a positive trend in an observational scatter plot establish by itself?**

- **A.** An association in the recorded data
- **B.** A guaranteed intervention effect
- **C.** The absence of hidden causes

:::{dropdown} Show the answer
**A. An association in the recorded data**

The plot describes how recorded values vary together. Causal effects require further assumptions or suitable experimental evidence beyond the visual trend.
:::

:::{caution} Scope and assumptions
Axis choices and restricted samples can change the apparent pattern. Always inspect the data definition.
:::
